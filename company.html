About Keydar

Keydar is a digital studio focused on building interactive products, fantasy universes, digital communication systems, and long-term creative and technical projects.

Keydar works in the following areas:

  • Software and game development
  • Worldbuilding and narrative design
  • Digital content production
  • Automation and communication tools
  • Creative and technical project development

The long-term Keydar ecosystem may include games, lore materials, books, interactive experiences, merchandise, and related digital services.
