Data Deletion Request

You may request deletion of personal data associated with your use of the Keydar website, communication channels, or related digital services.

How to Submit a Request

Send your request to:
info@keydar.com.ua

Please include:

  • Your contact email address or phone number used with the relevant service
  • A short description of the data or account concerned
  • The service or communication channel through which the data was provided
  • Any additional information reasonably required to identify the relevant record

Identity Verification

Before processing a deletion request, we may ask for reasonable information to verify the identity of the requester and confirm their connection to the relevant account or data. This is required to prevent unauthorized deletion.

Processing the Request

After verification, the request will be reviewed and applicable personal data will be deleted or anonymized unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, auditing, or compliance with applicable law.
