Data Deletion Request

Last updated: 27 July 2026

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your interaction with the Keydar website, communication channels, or related services.

How to submit a request

Send an email to info@keydar.com.ua with the subject Data Deletion Request.

Please include:

  • Your name and contact email
  • The phone number or account identifier connected to the request
  • A short description of the data or service involved
  • Any other information reasonably necessary to locate the data

Verification and processing

To prevent unauthorized deletion, we may ask you to verify your identity or control of the relevant phone number, email address, or account. Do not send passwords, payment card details, identity documents, or other sensitive information unless specifically requested through an appropriate secure process.

After verification, the request will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal, security, accounting, dispute-resolution, and fraud-prevention requirements.

Questions

Questions about data handling may also be sent to info@keydar.com.ua.
